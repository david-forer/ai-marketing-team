# Kickoff questions for {{COMPANY}}

Prepared for {{FOUNDER}}. These take about 45 minutes on a call, and any answer you don't know yet is useful too.

## People

- Who will run this each day? Most of their time goes to reading drafts and fixing them, so a sharp editor fits well.
- Who approves anything that goes out under the company name?
- Who handles design, in house or outside?
- Who holds the admin login for Google Workspace or Microsoft 365?

## Customers and proof

- Which 3 customers would you clone if you could?
- Which customers have agreed to let you use their name?
- Which numbers would you be comfortable defending in front of a buyer?
- Is there anything you must not claim, for legal or licensing reasons?

## Tools

- Where do sales calls get recorded, and are transcripts saved?
- Where do support conversations live: a shared inbox, a help desk or someone's personal email?
- Which ad platforms do you run, and who can log in to them?
- Do you use a website visitor tool? If you do, does your privacy policy mention it?

## Rules

- Do your calls tell people they're being recorded?
- Does anyone in your customer base have a contract that limits what you can do with their data?
- What should never be sent to an AI tool, such as client financials or health records?
- Who has to be told before staff see agents in their tools?
