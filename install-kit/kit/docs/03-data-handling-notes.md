# Data handling notes for {{COMPANY}}

This is a working checklist, written by a consultant and not a lawyer. Have {{COMPANY}}'s counsel confirm anything marked as a legal question before the agent that depends on it goes live.

## Before any data connects

- Read the Claude plan terms for {{COMPANY}}'s plan. Anthropic's pricing page lists no model training on content by default for Team and Enterprise, so confirm that in the agreement {{COMPANY}} signs.
- Agree in writing what must never go to an AI tool.
- Check customer contracts for limits on how their data gets used.
- Keep the Brain folder and all connected accounts in {{COMPANY}}'s name.

## Call recordings (legal question)

Some US states require everyone on a call to consent to recording. Agents 1 and 6 use transcripts, so confirm that callers are told about recording, and that the notice covers the states {{COMPANY}} sells into.

## Customer quotes

Internal use of a quote needs no extra step here. Public use needs the customer's permission, recorded in the Offer and proof doc with the date.

## Website visitor tools (legal question)

Agent 7 depends on identifying companies that visit the website. Update the privacy policy to cover this, and confirm whether state privacy laws apply.

## Outreach email (legal question)

US commercial email rules include accurate sender details, a working opt-out and a physical address. Agent 7 only writes drafts, and {{COMPANY}} is the sender of every email, so counsel should confirm these rules for {{COMPANY}}. Other countries have stricter rules, so confirm before writing to anyone outside the US.

## When someone leaves

Remove their Claude seat and connector access on their last day. Remove the consultant's seat at handover and record the date in the tracker.
