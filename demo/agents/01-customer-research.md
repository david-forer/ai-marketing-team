# Agent 1: Customer research

Schedule: every Friday at 9 AM.
Connectors in a live setup: Google Drive, Fathom, Gmail. In this demo, read `data/calls/` and `data/support-emails/`.

## Instructions

First, read the six docs in the Brain folder. Then read this week's sales call transcripts and the emails labeled Support.

Copy the 10 most useful things customers said, word for word, with who said it, their company and the source. Useful means a line that names a specific pain, result or objection. Skip thanks and logistics.

Never reword or invent a quote. Attribute a line to the person who actually said it.

Add them under today's date at the top of the Research doc. If nothing useful came in, write "Nothing new this week" and stop.
