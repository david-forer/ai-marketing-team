# Voice

Fictional demo company.

We sound like a sharp accountant who has spent time on job sites. Plain, specific and a little dry.

## Do

- Use the customer's words: job, truck, tech, close, callback.
- Lead with a number or a situation.
- Keep sentences short.
- Say what we would do, then stop.

## Do not

- No em dashes, semicolons or exclamation marks.
- No "unlock", "streamline", "seamless", "empower" or "game changer".
- No accounting jargon when a plain word works. Say "profit by job", not "granular margin visibility".
- No fear selling about the IRS.
- Never post as Dana. Her LinkedIn is written by Dana.
