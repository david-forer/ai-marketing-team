# Offer and proof

Fictional demo company. All names, numbers and quotes are invented.

## The company

Fenwick Row Bookkeeping, Columbus, Ohio. Founded by Dana Okafor, CPA. 13 staff, $2.6M revenue, 61 clients, all in the trades. No marketing or operations lead. Dana still approves everything that goes out.

## What we sell

| Plan | Price | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Core Books | $1,400 per month | Monthly close by day 10, field software to QuickBooks reconciliation |
| Job Cost | $2,600 per month | Core Books plus profit by job, technician and service line |
| Fractional Controller | $5,200 per month | Job Cost plus monthly review with Dana, cash forecast and pricing support |

The first step for every buyer is a free 30 minute Job Cost Check. A booked Job Cost Check counts as a sign-up.

## Proof

- Median close moved from day 31 to day 8 across the 61 clients.
- Summit Trail Heating and Cooling found $84,000 a year in underpriced maintenance agreements in the first quarter.
- Halvorsen Mechanical became a client in October 2026, too recent to quote results.
- 96 percent of clients stayed through the last 12 months.
