# Customers

Fictional demo company. All names, numbers and quotes are invented.

## Who we sell to

Owners of home service contractors in the US: HVAC, plumbing and electrical. Revenue of $2M to $20M, one to five locations, 10 to 80 staff. They run the field side on ServiceTitan or Housecall Pro and the books on QuickBooks Online.

## Who decides

The owner decides. The office manager or the owner's spouse usually does the books today, and they are the person who feels the pain first. They often book the call.

## What they say is wrong

- They learn a job lost money weeks after it finished.
- The field software and QuickBooks disagree, and nobody knows which one is right.
- The CPA shows up at tax time and reports on the past.
- They cannot say which technician, truck or service line makes money.
- Month end closes late, or does not close at all.

## What they want

A number they trust by the second week of the month, and the ability to price the next job from it.

## Common objections

- "I already have a CPA."
- "We tried an outsourced bookkeeper and they did not understand the trades."
- "Moving everything over sounds like a nightmare during heating season."
