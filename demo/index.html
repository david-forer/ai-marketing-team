Demo. Fenwick Row Bookkeeping is fictional, and so is every name, number and quote here.

Three people and eight agents run the marketing

One working week at a 13-person bookkeeping firm for HVAC, plumbing and electrical contractors, with no marketing hire.

Founder

Sets the strategy and says yes or no.

Operator

Writes the instructions and reads everything before it goes out.

Designer

Owns the brand, builds the templates and approves every ad.

The week

Every agent reads the same six docs first: customers, offer and proof, voice, competitors, wins and flops, and research. Open any agent to see what it produced.

Friday: listen

1. Customer research

Read 6 calls and 10 support emails, kept 10 quotes
I find out I lost money on a job six weeks after the van left the driveway.

Greta Halvorsen, Owner, Halvorsen Mechanical. Quotes are word for word, and a script checks each one against its source.

2. Competitor research

Monday: decide and write

3. Weekly ideas

20 ideas, each tied to a real quote or a past win

"Your office manager should not be your tiebreaker" comes from a customer quote. If an idea has no source, the agent drops it. The founder keeps or kills each one in a spreadsheet.

4. Channel writer

Wrote the kept ideas for LinkedIn, email, blog and ads
Fast books are not the same as right books.

The first line of a company LinkedIn post. The writer never posts as the founder, and it can only use numbers from the proof doc.

Tuesday to Thursday: sell

5. Ad maker

Built a 4-row sheet for Canva Bulk Create

One headline per row, with a template picked for each. The designer uploads the sheet and approves every ad.

6. Sales deck

Type in a company, get a 7-slide PowerPoint

Built for Redbird Electric, with a quote from a paying electrical client and only numbers from the proof doc. Download the deck.

7. Outreach

4 pricing-page alerts, 2 skipped, 3 drafts

It skipped a college and a 41-second visit. The drafts never mention the visit, and the operator hits send.

Many electrical contractors can tell you what they billed last month but not what service work kept compared with new construction.
